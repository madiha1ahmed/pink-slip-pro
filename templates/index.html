Welcome to PinkSlip Pro

PinkSlip Pro is a streamlined digital solution for tracking missed homework. Assign a pink slip when a student misses an assignment, and an email is instantly sent to the parent. When a student receives three pink slips, an automatic detailed report is sent to both the parent and homeroom teacher. Each pink slip is also archived two months after it's issued. No more paper. Better record-keeping. Timely communication.
